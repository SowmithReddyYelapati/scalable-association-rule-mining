APRIORI CONFIGURATION

Configure Parameters

Adjust the mining parameters to control how many patterns are discovered.

Minimum Support

Minimum frequency required for an itemset to be considered frequent.

2.0%
0.1% 10%
💡

Higher support finds only very common product combinations. Lower support discovers less frequent patterns.

Minimum Confidence

Minimum reliability required for an association rule.

60%
10% 100%
💡

Higher confidence produces more reliable rules but may reduce the number of rules.

ⓘ
Lift is calculated automatically

Lift measures how strongly two products are associated compared with what would be expected if they were independent. It is calculated for every rule and available as a result metric.

WHAT HAPPENS NEXT?

From transactions to useful rules

Find frequent itemsets

2

3
