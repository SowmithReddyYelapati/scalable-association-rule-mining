Set Mining Parameters

Configure the statistical frequency and conditional probability thresholds.

Minimum Support

Range: 0.1% – 10.0%

Minimum frequency of occurrence required across all transactions.

{{ "%.1f"|format((analysis.support or 0.02) * 100) }}%
0.1% (Broad discovery) 10.0% (Strict frequency)
💡

Lower support uncovers niche combinations; higher support restricts results to dominant basket items.

Minimum Confidence

Range: 10% – 100%

Minimum conditional probability required for an association rule: P(Consequent | Antecedent).

{{ "%.0f"|format((analysis.confidence or 0.60) * 100) }}%
10% (Permissive rules) 100% (Absolute correlation)
💡

Higher confidence yields more reliable rules by demanding stronger antecedent-to-consequent co-occurrence.

Rules below this lift value will be filtered out.

ⓘ Lift > 1 indicates positive association (products bought together more frequently than independent chance).
