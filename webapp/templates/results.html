STATISTICAL REFERENCE

How to Interpret ARM Metrics

Understanding the mathematical definitions and practical meaning of Support, Confidence, and Lift.

Support: \(P(A \cup B)\) Proportion of transactions in the dataset containing all items in the rule. Evaluates statistical frequency. Support(A→B) = count(A ∪ B) / |T|
Confidence: \(P(B | A)\) Conditional probability that a transaction contains B given that it already contains A. Evaluates rule reliability. Confidence(A→B) = Support(A ∪ B) / Support(A)
Lift: \(\frac{P(B|A)}{P(B)}\) Ratio of observed joint support to expected support under independence. Lift > 1 indicates positive association. Lift(A→B) = Confidence(A→B) / Support(B)
