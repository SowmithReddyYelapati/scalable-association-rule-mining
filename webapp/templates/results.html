INTERPRETING LIFT

How to read the Lift metric

Lift measures how much stronger the association is compared to random co-occurrence.

▲ Lift > 1 — Positive association The products appear together more often than expected by chance. A higher lift means a stronger, more useful rule. Lift = Confidence / P(Consequent)
= Lift = 1 — Independent The items occur together purely by chance. The rule has no predictive value.
▼ Lift < 1 — Negative association The items appear together less often than expected. Buying one product makes the other less likely.
METRIC REFERENCE

Support · Confidence · Lift

Support The proportion of all transactions that contain both the antecedent and consequent. Support(A→B) = freq(A∪B) / |T|
Confidence Among transactions containing the antecedent, the fraction that also contain the consequent. Confidence(A→B) = Support(A∪B) / Support(A)
Lift How much more likely the consequent is given the antecedent, relative to its overall frequency. Lift(A→B) = Confidence(A→B) / Support(B)
