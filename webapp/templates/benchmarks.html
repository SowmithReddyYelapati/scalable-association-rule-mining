SCALABILITY

Why scalability matters

Association rule mining can be computationally expensive. Understanding the trade-offs helps choose the right algorithm and parameters.

Candidate Generation Apriori repeatedly generates candidate itemsets from previously discovered frequent itemsets. The number of candidates can grow exponentially at low support thresholds.
Multiple Database Scans Traditional Apriori requires one full database scan per itemset size k. This is expensive on very large transaction datasets.
Support Threshold Effect Lowering the threshold by even 1% can produce dramatically more candidates and frequent itemsets, increasing runtime nonlinearly.
